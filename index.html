Featured case study · Digital health

FitFuel

A privacy-first fitness and nutrition dashboard that helps people track meals, workouts, hydration, weight, and patterns in their own data—all within the browser.

My contributionUser research · Product design · Data analysis
TimelineJanuary 2026

Opportunity

Bring everyday wellness signals into one understandable view while keeping personal data under the user’s control.

Research

Conducted user interviews to identify wellness-tracking needs and inform a user-centered product direction.

Product

The experience includes flows for nutrition, exercise, hydration, weight trends, daily goals, and personalized pattern summaries.

Privacy

Designed a zero-backend experience: no account, trackers, or server storage. Users can export, import, or delete their data.

Interaction

Added “Ask Your Data,” a deterministic question interface that answers common questions without an LLM, API key, or network call.

Implementation

The project is an installable offline-first web app with browser storage and dependency-free SVG progress charts.
